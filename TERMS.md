# Melo — Terms of Use

**Effective date:** October 28, 2026

## About Melo

Melo is a vocabulary app and connected service operated by Zui Chen. It lets you
save selected words, retrieve and search your vocabulary, and request changes or
deletions through supported AI clients. The iOS app provides vocabulary review.

The iOS software is also subject to the license terms presented on its App Store
listing. Your use of an AI client is subject to that provider's own terms.

## Your account and content

Use Melo only for lawful purposes and only with accounts and content you are
authorized to access. Keep your personal MCP URL and connection credentials
private. They allow access to your vocabulary. Do not use or share someone
else's credentials.

You retain your rights in the content you provide. You authorize Melo to process
and store that content as needed to operate the service and carry out your
requests. Only save vocabulary and examples needed for your learning; avoid
including sensitive personal information.

## Saving and deleting vocabulary

The connector saves vocabulary when you request it or explicitly enable
auto-save. Your connected client may ask for confirmation before taking an
action. Saving an existing word can replace its stored definition and fields.

Deleting a word removes that entry. The Delete Account action and the
delete_account tool remove the account's saved vocabulary. As explained in the
privacy policy, vocabulary deletion does not currently revoke all connection
records or invalidate the Apple-provided account token.

## Accuracy and availability

Vocabulary details supplied by AI can be inaccurate. Check meanings,
pronunciation, translations, and inflections when accuracy matters.

Melo's availability and features may change. Keep an export of vocabulary you
want to preserve. The service does not promise uninterrupted operation or that
AI-generated vocabulary details will be correct.

## Privacy and stopping use

The [privacy policy](https://azuic.github.io/melo-privacy/) describes what Melo
stores, its service providers, retention, and deletion options.

You can stop using Melo and remove its connection from your AI client at any
time. You can delete saved vocabulary using the app or supported tools. Contact
hi@zui.ooo for help with remaining connection records; do not email credentials.
